Frequently asked questions

Q. Why does Excel show my CSV as garbled text?
A. Japanese editions of Excel read CSV files as Shift_JIS by default, while most modern web systems export CSV as UTF-8. That mismatch is what produces the garbled text.
Q. What is UTF-8 with BOM?
A. A BOM (byte order mark) is a small marker placed at the start of a UTF-8 file. It tells Excel that the file really is UTF-8, so the text displays correctly instead of turning into mojibake.
